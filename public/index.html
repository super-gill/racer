How it works

Your pod has two afterburning jet engines, one lever each. Keys move the levers and they stay where you leave them. The engines take a moment to spool up to what the levers ask for. You turn by running one engine harder than the other, so turning always costs speed. Below idle a lever deploys that engine's thrust reverser: that is your brake. A running jet always pushes a little, so you never fully stop.

Linked levers (default)

W / UpPush both levers forward (they stay there)
S / DownPull both levers back, past zero into reverse
A D / Left RightTurn: pulls the inside lever back while held

Manual levers [M to switch]

W / SLeft lever forward / back
Up / DownRight lever forward / back

Both schemes

SpaceBoth levers to full instantly
XCut both levers to zero
ShiftAfterburner: only with the lever at full and the engine spooled up
BackspaceRecover to the track if you get stuck
Engines run on the grid: spool up during the countdown.

Heat and fuel

Revs heat the engines and the afterburner heats them fast. Past the red line an engine flames out and has to relight. Everything burns fuel from one tank, the afterburner heavily, and a full tank makes the pod heavier. Run dry and everything shuts down: you are out, and at speed the pod hits the ground hard.
